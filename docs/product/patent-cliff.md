# Patent-cliff urgency

**Inject:** INJ-004 (patent-cliff urgency).  
**Obligation:** commercial urgency never waives a GxP gate. A request marked urgent produces the same regulated pack as one that is not, excluding only fields that record priority.

## The pressure

Portfolio and forecast fixtures can mark a product as commercially exposed. That is evidence of pressure. It is not a reason to skip a contradiction, collapse an abstention, or let a model decide.

## What the product does

BR-135 / AC-FR005-17: extreme urgency, deadline, and commercial-exposure fields produce a pack **byte-identical** to the run with those fields absent, except fields that record priority. The engines do not read urgency as licence.

`specs/product/scope.md` §4 remains absolute. Urgency is not an exception list.

## Stop if the claim drifts

A pack, annotation, or console control that treats “urgent” as authority to release, confirm a signal, or move stock is a defect against this artefact and against plan §14 R-08.
